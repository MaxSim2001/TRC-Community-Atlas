[`LICENSE.txt`](../LICENSE.txt).

This is a custom source-available license. It is not an Open Source Initiative
approved open-source license because it restricts resale and certain commercial
offerings centered on Atlas. This page is an explanatory summary only.

## Why a custom license is used

Existing licenses did not exactly match the intended model:

- [PolyForm Noncommercial](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0/)
  excludes ordinary commercial use by businesses and MSPs;
- [PolyForm Shield](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/shield/1.0.0/) uses a
  broad competition test that does not clearly separate permitted MSP services
  from a competing Atlas product;
- [Elastic License 2.0](https://www.elastic.co/licensing/elastic-license/faq/)
  can be ambiguous when a customer's personnel access a hosted instance;
- [Business Source License](https://mariadb.com/bsl-faq-mariadb/) requires a
  future change to an open-source license, which is not the selected model.

The Atlas license therefore distinguishes paid IT services from commercializing
the software itself.

## Free use by MSPs

An MSP may use Atlas free of charge in professional activities without limits
on clients, technicians, or instances. An MSP may:

- document, administer, and manage client environments;
- install or host Atlas on its own infrastructure or a client's infrastructure;
- allow authorized client personnel to access their instance;
- modify the source for internal needs or client-specific internal needs;
- charge for time, expertise, infrastructure, installation, configuration,
  migration, training, support, maintenance, and backups;
- use Atlas while delivering paid IT or managed services.

These services remain permitted when Atlas access is incidental to a broader
professional or managed-services relationship, Atlas is not sold or separately
priced, and the offering is not marketed as a standalone hosted Atlas product.

## Examples

| Scenario | Status |
| --- | --- |
| A business installs Atlas for internal documentation | Free and permitted |
| An MSP documents 200 clients in Atlas | Free and permitted |
| An MSP charges for installation, training, and maintenance | Permitted |
| An MSP hosts one instance for a client under a broader IT agreement | Permitted when Atlas remains incidental and is not priced as a product |
| A person shares an unmodified copy with the license and notices | Permitted |
| A company sells the Atlas ZIP or an Atlas software license | Prohibited without written permission |
| A third party renames Atlas and sells the modified version | Prohibited without written permission |
| A vendor sells a subscription whose primary product is hosted Atlas | Prohibited without written permission |
| A distributor removes TheRisingCloud copyright notices | Prohibited |

## Redistribution and modifications

Permitted copies must retain the license, copyright, and other notices. A
distributed modified version must clearly identify its changes, remain under
the same license, and provide corresponding source code at no charge to the
same recipients.

Third-party components remain governed by their own licenses.

## Scope of the license change

This license applies to Atlas versions distributed with the new text from its
effective date. Copies of older versions already received under PolyForm
Noncommercial remain governed by the terms delivered with those copies. An
official Release should identify the first version shipped under the current
license.

## Legal review

The custom license reflects the project's intended permissions and
restrictions, but it has not been presented as a standard license approved by a
licensing organization. TheRisingCloud does not make legal review a prerequisite
for publication or use. Independent legal review can still reduce ambiguity
about jurisdiction, liability limits, contributor rights, and the line between
permitted MSP services and a prohibited commercial Atlas offering.
